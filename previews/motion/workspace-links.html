Proposed behaviour

  • Same full folder path, across both providers. Separate worktrees remain separate.
  • Keep the existing list order. Wires follow the rows as they expand, move or scroll.
  • Only groups with two or more listed threads get a wire. Unknown folders stay unlinked.
  • Hover or keyboard focus highlights a connection. Context wheels and row actions keep their existing roles.
